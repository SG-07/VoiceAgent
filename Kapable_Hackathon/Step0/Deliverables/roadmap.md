# Roadmap: Speech Coach LMS (working title)

> DRAFT: awaiting approval.

## Hackathon demo (thin slice, build first)
- Learner logs in (email and password)
- Record in the browser or upload audio for an assignment
- Audio saved, then transcribed (Groq Whisper)
- AI scores four competencies on the 1-4 rubric with explanation and suggestion
- Toast message when feedback is ready
- Progress page for one learner across seeded past recordings

## v1 (must-have, ship first for a pilot)
- Course modules and materials for learners
- Baseline, mid-course and final assessment tracking
- Instructor view of submissions with AI analysis and instructor feedback shown next to it
- Batch-level analytics for instructors
- Badges and streaks
- Learner deletion requests
- Automatic retry of failed AI runs
- Review data location and privacy terms before real learner data (no requirement for the demo)
- Manual setup of each client, batch and learner by the team

## v2 (next)
- Admin self-service to upload courses, create batches and manage learners
- Email notifications
- Instructor tools for calibrating AI scores against their own
- Customisable rubrics per client
- Mobile-optimised recording improvements

## v3 (later / nice-to-have)
- Live and two-person role-play assessment (negotiation, conflict)
- Video analysis for body language and eye contact
- Professional judgement and reflection assessment
- Weighted grades and certification, once the course designer agrees
- Social login and company single sign-on
