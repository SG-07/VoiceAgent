# Users: Speech Coach LMS (working title)

> DRAFT: awaiting approval. Personas are composites from the interview, not named individuals.

## Persona 1: Learner (working professional)
- Context: mid-career professional in a batch of 100-150 on a communication course, preparing for senior management roles. Records spoken assignments on a laptop or phone.
- Goal: get better at presenting and communicating, and see evidence of improvement.
- Frustration today: waits 1-2 weeks for feedback and has often repeated the same habits in later assignments by the time it arrives. Has no view of progress over time.
- Why this product helps: feedback within minutes against a clear rubric, plus a dashboard and badges that make progress visible and motivating.

## Persona 2: Instructor (trainer at the training company)
- Context: reviews recordings from one or more batches of 100-150 learners.
- Goal: give useful, personal coaching to every learner and notice who is struggling.
- Frustration today: listening to every recording one by one takes time, so feedback is slow and instructors have no batch-level data.
- Why this product helps: an AI first-pass analysis next to each recording, so time goes into coaching, plus batch analytics.

## Persona 3: Training company (the buyer)
- Context: runs multiple batches of a communication course it has already built, with no LMS of its own.
- Goal: deliver the course and scale feedback without hiring more instructors.
- Frustration today: no platform for course material, assignments or analytics.
- Why this product helps: one place for course material, AI-assisted assessment and progress data. In v1 the team sets up each client manually.
