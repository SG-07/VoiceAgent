# Vision: Speech Coach LMS (working title)

> DRAFT: awaiting approval.

In two years, training companies that teach communication run their courses on this platform instead of a patchwork of shared folders and email. A learner records an assignment and has rubric-based feedback within minutes, while the speaking habits they are working on are still fresh. Their dashboard shows concrete evidence of what has improved, from their baseline to the end of the course.

Instructors no longer spend their hours on first-pass listening. The AI gives each recording a consistent first assessment against the course's own rubric, and instructors use that to give sharper, more personal coaching, and to spot learners who need help early. The one-to-two-week feedback delay no longer exists, and repeating mistakes across assignments is no longer built into the course design.

The platform grows from solo audio assignments toward live role-plays, video and body language, and tools for training companies to set up their own courses, batches and rubrics without our help.
